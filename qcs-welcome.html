2

Enter Your Galaxy AI Key

Once inside your dashboard you will see the Galaxy AI Key field at the top. Enter your gx_ API key and click Save. This connects the generator to the Galaxy AI engine — without it no images can be created.

Important: Your key is stored in your browser session only — it is never saved publicly or shared. You will need to re-enter it each time you open a new browser session.
3

Generate Your First Horror Image

Click the Horror Studio tab. Select your horror style — Gothic, Supernatural, Psychological, Creature, Apocalyptic or Slasher. Write your prompt describing the scene you want. Choose your dimensions and lighting. Hit Generate.

Pro prompt tip: Be specific and descriptive. Instead of "a scary house" try "a decaying Victorian mansion on a cliff edge, blood red sky, fog rolling through iron gates, skeletal hands reaching from the earth below." The more detail you give Galaxy AI the better the result.

Pro Tips for Dark Creators

Get the most out of Ques Creator Studio with these insider tips.

✍️

Write Detailed Prompts

The more specific your prompt the better Galaxy AI performs. Describe lighting, mood, setting, time of day, weather and specific elements you want to see.

🎨

Mix Styles

Try combining style keywords in your prompt. Gothic architecture with creature elements. Psychological horror with apocalyptic landscapes. Experiment and discover your signature look.

📐

Match Size to Platform

Always choose your dimensions based on where the image will be used. YouTube needs landscape, Shorts need portrait, social posts need square. Get it right from the start.

🔄

Regenerate and Refine

Your first generation is rarely your best. Use the Regenerate button to get variations on the same prompt. Small tweaks to your prompt create dramatically different results.

💀

Build a Content Library

Generate and download images in batches. Build up a library of horror content you can use across multiple videos, posts and campaigns without having to create from scratch every time.

🔗

Promote While You Create

Every time you use the studio you are experiencing exactly what your referrals will experience. Share that experience authentically and your affiliate link will convert naturally.
